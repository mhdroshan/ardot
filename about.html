Who We Are

Crafting Meaningful Spaces
Across South India

Ardot is a full-service interior design firm based in Nilambur, dedicated to creating spaces that balance beauty, purpose, and comfort. With years of experience, we’ve worked on diverse projects across South India — from luxury homes, cafés, and restaurants to schools, colleges, offices, and medical facilities. Our multidisciplinary team brings together architectural planning, space styling, and project management to craft interiors that truly reflect each client’s vision and lifestyle.

02
What we do?

Relationships come first,
everything else follows

At Ardot, we believe every space begins with understanding the people who live and work within it. Our design process is built on strong relationships and open collaboration — because great interiors are born from trust and shared vision. From concept to completion, we work closely with our clients to design residential and commercial spaces that feel personal, functional, and enduring. Whether it’s a modern home, a cozy café, or a large institutional project, we ensure every detail reflects purpose, quality, and connection.

Contact us today for your free consultation.
